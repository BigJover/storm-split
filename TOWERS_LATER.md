## Meteor Beacon — "the extinction tower"

A radio mast with a red flare. Every 12s it calls a meteor onto the densest spot of track:
big area damage, burning crater. Ground only. Breaks 1 at base (area), but its Impact path
gets pierce-through on the centre.
**Why 4×:** the biggest area hit in the game and the theme's own joke (the hunters call in
the extinction). **Weakness:** slow, no air, wasted on spread-out waves.

| Path | Focus | T1 | T2 | T3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Impact** | centre damage, breaks | Heavier Rock | Iron Core | *Ground Zero* (the centre breaks 2) |
| **Shower** | more, smaller meteors | Split Rock | Twin Fall | *Meteor Shower Lite* (5 small meteors over 2s) |
| **Aftermath** | crater, burn, control | Hot Crater | Ash Cloud (dinos in it miss their attacks 30%) | *Shockwave Ring* (knockback ring, not bosses) |

**Impact T4:** A *Planet Killer Core* (centre breaks 3, ×2 vs bosses). B *Homing Flare* (aims
at the biggest dino instead of the densest spot).
**Impact T5:** A **Extinction Event** — once a round (button in the panel), a giant meteor:
everything on screen takes ×20, the centre breaks 4. B **Dino Killer** — every meteor drops
the biggest dino hit 2 sizes outright. C **Impact Winter** — after each meteor, all dinos on
the map slowed 30% for 3s.

**Shower T4:** A *Endless Rain* (a meteor every 4s, smaller). B *Scatter Field* (meteors spread
along the whole track).
**Shower T5:** A **Starfall** — 20 meteors across the map every 15s. B **Comet Tail** — a
comet sweeps the full track length once a round. C **Shooting Stars** — meteors hit air too.

**Aftermath T4:** A *Tar and Ash* (craters stay 8s). B *Glass Crater* (craters strip armour).
**Aftermath T5:** A **Fire Rain** — burning ground along every crater, merges into one
stretch. B **Dust Storm** — dinos in the ash can't bite or shoot. C **New Ice Age** — the
crater freezes instead of burning: 2s hard stop, bosses half.

## Amber Resin Cannon — entomb in amber

Fires globs of sticky resin. A hit dino is slowed; enough resin and it's **entombed in
amber** for a few seconds (a hard stop, glowing orange). An entombed dino at its last size
becomes a **fossil**: removed, and pays a bonus.
**Why 4×:** the strongest hard control in the game plus an economy twist, perfectly on theme
(Amber is the currency). **Weakness:** low raw damage; bosses resist entombing.

| Path | Focus | T1 | T2 | T3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Entomb** | hard stops | Thick Resin | Quick Set | *Amber Tomb* (entomb 2s) |
| **Fossilize** | cash and take-downs | Collector's Eye | Rare Find (+cash per fossil) | *Fossil Record* (last-two-size dinos can fossilize) |
| **Sap Spray** | area | Wide Nozzle | Sap Mist | *Sap Flood* (resin pool on the track, slows 40%) |

**Entomb T4:** A *Hardened Amber* (entombed dinos take +30% from everything). B *Chain Tomb*
(entombing spreads to one neighbour).
**Entomb T5:** A **Amber Age** — every 20s every dino in range is entombed 2s (bosses 1s).
B **Living Amber** — an entombed boss is held 3s once per boss. C **Time Capsule** — the
entombed dino is sent back 15 studs up the track.

**Fossilize T4:** A *Museum Piece* (fossils pay ×2). B *Dig Crew* (fossils drop a chest).
**Fossilize T5:** A **Fossil Fuel** — each fossil gives +5% attack speed to towers in range for
the round (cap +30%). B **Jurassic Archive** — fossils count double for bounties.
C **Amber Rush** — every 25 fossils in a match pay 1 Amber to every hunter (capped, under the
"logging in never beats playing" rules).

**Sap Spray T4:** A *Sticky Floor* (the pool also stops bites). B *Resin Rain* (hits air).
**Sap Spray T5:** A **Sap Sea** — the pool covers the track in range. B **Resin Wall** — once a
round, a wall of amber on the track holds everything for 3s. C **Sweet Trap** — dinos
are drawn to the pool (Pteranodons dive into it).

## Ranger Station — NPC hunters

A cabin that trains **rangers**: AI hunters who walk the track against the dinos, shoot,
and die if bitten too much, then respawn at the station. The station takes damage; rangers
have HP.
**Why 4×:** the only tower that fights *on* the track and moves; feels like extra teammates
in a small lobby. **Weakness:** rangers can be trampled; the station needs repairs.

| Path | Focus | T1 | T2 | T3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Riflemen** | ranged rangers | Recruit | Sharpshooter | *Squad* (3 rangers) |
| **Trackers** | rangers that mark and set traps | Snare Kit | Bait | *Bear Traps* (stun the first dino over them) |
| **Wardens** | tougher rangers | Leather Coats | Medic Bag | *Shield Wall* (rangers take 40% less) |

**Riflemen T4:** A *Elite Squad* (5 rangers). B *Big Bore Ranger* (one ranger, breaks 3).
**Riflemen T5:** A **Ranger Corps** — 8 rangers in formation. B **Legend of the Park** — one
hero-grade ranger with its own ability (a Tracking Dart). C **Patrol Jeep** — rangers ride a
jeep along the track, firing.

**Trackers T4:** A *Pit Traps* (traps sink last-size dinos). B *Lure Calls* (dinos stop to
fight rangers 1s).
**Trackers T5:** A **Master Trapper** — traps along the whole track every round.
B **Net Launchers** — rangers net a boss 2s, once a boss. C **Game Warden** — every
ranger take-down marks nearby dinos +20%.

**Wardens T4:** A *Field Rations* (rangers heal between rounds). B *Rally Point* (rangers
respawn 2× faster).
**Wardens T5:** A **Last Rangers** — rangers can't die during a boss round. B **Bodyguards**
— rangers stand by towers and take hits for them. C **Hunter's Lodge** — hunters in range
respawn here, half time.

## Spotter Blimp — can't be damaged

An airship that circles the whole map with a door gunner. It flies, so **dinos can't hit
it** ("somewhat weaker" in raw numbers, like the 2× batch's untouchables). Global range.
**Why 4×:** covers the whole map, never needs a repair, and helps hunters see. **Weakness:**
moderate damage, it circles (gaps in coverage).

| Path | Focus | T1 | T2 | T3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Door Gun** | damage | Belt Gun | Twin Mount | *Strafing Run* (a line of fire along the track) |
| **Searchlight** | support | Spotlight (dinos in light take +10%) | Wide Beam | *Night Hunt* (towers in the light +15% range) |
| **Cargo** | drops | Supply Crates (like Air Drop) | Ammo Drop | *Med Drop* (heals hunters below) |

**Door Gun T4:** A *Heavy Mount* (pierces armour, breaks 2). B *Rocket Pods* (splash).
**Door Gun T5:** A **Gunship** — circles faster with two guns and rockets. B **Bombing Run** —
once a round, bombs the full track length. C **Sky Hook** — snatches a last-size dino off
the track every 5s.

**Searchlight T4:** A *Floodlights* (light the whole circle). B *Signal Lamp* (hunters in the
light reload 20% faster).
**Searchlight T5:** A **Eye of the Storm Blimp** — marks every dino under it +30%. B **Lighthouse**
— towers anywhere get +10% damage while the blimp is overhead. C **Spotter's Map** — hunters
see every dino on the minimap/outline (dinos only; PvP-safe).

**Cargo T4:** A *Heavy Cargo* (bigger crates). B *Rebuild Kits* (repairs a trampled tower below
it each lap).
**Cargo T5:** A **Supply Chain Blimp** — a crate every lap. B **Paratroopers** — drops 2
rangers each round (if Ranger Station is picked, they share a look). C **Airlift** — once a
round, lifts a trampled tower and puts it down repaired.
